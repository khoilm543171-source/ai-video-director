# Music Direction Skill

Use once per episode, not once per scene.

## Prompt fields
- genre / sonic identity;
- mood;
- instrumentation;
- BPM;
- duration;
- instrumental=true;
- energy arc tied to story beats.

Default series identity:
playful miniature cinematic adventure, light percussion,
subtle industrial texture, warm educational tone.

## Arc
- hook: light curiosity;
- problem discovery: modest energy rise;
- explanation: sparse and unobtrusive;
- final interview answer: gentle resolution.

## Rules
- No vocals.
- Do not restart the music every scene.
- Keep arrangement sparse under technical dialogue.
- Duck music under speech during final mix.
- Prefer continuity over dramatic trailer-style impacts.
